Crop Selection

Live forecast is a proposed feature.

A soil photograph alone cannot reliably determine pH, nutrients or full soil composition. It is stored only as a reference here and is not used in scoring. Use a laboratory soil test for accurate analysis.

Growth of Crop

Tip: take a close photo of one leaf in daylight.

This prototype measures colour only. A photograph alone cannot definitively diagnose every disease or nutrient deficiency. If symptoms are unclear, consult an agricultural expert or your local Krishi office.
